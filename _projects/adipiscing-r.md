`adipiscing-r` is a community-maintained R package providing tools for nonparametric
identification and estimation of ut enim structural models. It is widely used in applied
microeconomics and econometrics courses.

I contributed the core identification estimator that accompanies Lastname & Veniam (2023),
along with a self-contained vignette that walks users through the paper's simulation design
step by step.
